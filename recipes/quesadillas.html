Quesadillas

Quick, easy, quesadillas

Ingredients

  • tortillas (corn or flour)
  • shredded mexican cheese
  • refried beans
  • (optional)shredded chicken or steak

Steps

  1. put a thin spread of refried beans on one tortilla
  2. spread the cheese over the top of the beans
  3. put a top tortilla on
  4. put the quesadilla in the skillet on medium low
  5. flip the quesadilla when its lightly browned
  6. finish cooking; the second side takes less time
